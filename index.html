BMI Index – BMI Calculator ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the BMI Index – BMI Calculator application.

1. Information We Collect

BMI Index – BMI Calculator does not require user registration or account creation.

The app does not collect or transmit personal information such as your email address, phone number, or precise location to our servers.

The app may use information entered by you, such as your name, age, weight, height, and gender, to provide BMI calculation and related features.

2. BMI Calculation Data

The information entered for BMI calculation is used to calculate and display your BMI, BMI category, healthy weight range, estimated ideal weight, and related BMI information.

BMI calculation information is stored locally on your device to provide features such as BMI History. This information is not uploaded to or stored on a remote server by the app.

3. Local Data Storage

Some information, such as your name and BMI calculation history, may be stored locally on your device using local application storage.

This locally stored information is used only to provide app features such as displaying your name and maintaining your BMI History.

The app does not maintain a user account or remotely store this information on our servers.

4. Share and Print Features

The app may provide options to share or print your BMI result. When you use these features, the information is handled by the sharing or printing service selected by you.

The app does not control how third-party sharing or printing services handle information.

5. Third-Party Services

The BMI calculation and local history features do not require the app to send your BMI information to our servers.

If third-party services are added in future versions, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain how those services handle information.

6. Children's Privacy

The BMI calculation features are intended for adult users. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Security

The app does not maintain a remote personal information database for BMI calculation data.

Information stored locally by the app remains on the user's device and may be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application, subject to the Android operating system's behavior.

8. Privacy Consent

Before using the application for the first time, users are shown a Privacy Policy notice and are asked to actively agree to this Privacy Policy.

Users can review this Privacy Policy before continuing to use the application.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the BMI Index – BMI Calculator application, you may contact us through the contact information provided on the application's store listing.
