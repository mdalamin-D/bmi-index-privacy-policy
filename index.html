Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

Developer: MD. AL AMIN

Health Calculator - BMI & More ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Health Calculator - BMI & More application.

1. Information We Collect

Health Calculator - BMI & More does not require user registration or account creation.

The app does not collect or transmit personal information such as your email address, phone number, or precise location to our servers.

The app may process information entered by you, such as your name, age, gender, weight, height, activity level, and climate selection to provide the health calculation features available in the app.

2. Health Calculators

Health Calculator - BMI & More provides several health and wellness calculation features, including:

  • BMI Calculator
  • Calorie Calculator
  • Body Fat Calculator
  • Water Intake Calculator

Information entered into these calculators is used to calculate and display estimated health and wellness results.

Depending on the calculator, the app may process information such as age, gender, weight, height, activity level, and climate selection.

The calculation data is stored locally on your device to provide features such as calculation history. The app does not upload or store this calculation data on a remote server.

3. Calculation History

The app provides a History feature that allows you to review your previous calculation results.

History information may include calculator type, date and time, age, gender, weight, height, activity level, climate selection, and calculated results.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers by the app.

You can delete your calculation history using the history management features available in the application.

4. Local Data Storage

Some information, such as your name, application preferences, and calculation history, may be stored locally on your device using local application storage.

Locally stored information is used only to provide app features such as displaying your name, saving preferences, maintaining calculation history, and providing calculator results.

The app does not maintain a user account or remotely store this information on our servers.

5. Health Insights and Information

The app may provide general health information, BMI categories, healthy weight ranges, estimated ideal weight information, calculator guides, and general health tips.

This information is provided for general informational and wellness purposes only.

The calculations and information provided by the app are estimates and should not be considered medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

If you have health concerns or need medical advice, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

6. Share and Print Features

The app may provide options to share or print certain calculation results.

When you use these features, the information is handled by the sharing or printing service selected by you.

The app does not control how third-party sharing or printing services handle information.

7. Third-Party Services

The health calculation and local history features do not require the app to send your calculation information to our servers.

If third-party services are used in the application, such as advertising or other external services, those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

We may update this Privacy Policy if third-party services are added, removed, or changed in future versions of the application.

8. Children's Privacy

The health calculation features are intended primarily for general wellness and adult users.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data Security

The app does not maintain a remote personal information database for health calculation data.

Information stored locally by the app remains on the user's device and may be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application, subject to the Android operating system's behavior.

10. Privacy Consent

Before using the application for the first time, users are shown a Privacy Policy notice and are asked to actively agree to this Privacy Policy.

Users can review this Privacy Policy before continuing to use the application.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the Health Calculator - BMI & More application, you may contact us through the contact information provided on the application's store listing.
