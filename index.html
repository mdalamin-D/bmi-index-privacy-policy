Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

BMI Index – BMI Calculator ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use our BMI calculator.

1. Information We Collect

BMI Index – BMI Calculator does not require user registration or account creation.

The app does not directly collect, store, or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The information you enter for BMI calculation, such as height, weight, age, and gender, is used to calculate and display your BMI result within the app.

2. BMI Calculation Data

The values entered by the user are used only for calculating BMI and displaying the result. The app does not require you to create an account or submit this information to us.

3. Share and Print Features

The app provides options to share or print your BMI result. When you use these features, the information is handled by the sharing or printing service selected by you. The app does not control how third-party sharing or printing services handle information.

4. Data Storage

BMI Index – BMI Calculator does not maintain a user account or a remote database for storing your BMI information.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version of the app does not directly collect personal information through its BMI calculation features.

If third-party services are added in future versions, this Privacy Policy may be updated to describe how those services handle data.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for users aged 18 and above. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Because the app does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information to us, we do not maintain a personal information database for BMI calculation.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the app, you may contact us through the contact information provided on the app's store listing.
